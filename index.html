Common Questions

Mainland vs Freezone?
Mainland allows you to trade anywhere in UAE without restrictions. Freezones are tax-free hubs usually focused on specific industries or export-heavy businesses.
How long does it take?
Initial license can take 60-120 minutes in some zones. Full setup with visas and bank accounts usually takes 2-4 weeks.
Ownership Rules?
Recent laws allow 100% foreign ownership for most commercial and industrial activities in the UAE Mainland.
