Common Questions

What is the difference between Mainland and Freezone?
A Mainland company allows you to trade anywhere inside the UAE and internationally without any restrictions. It is regulated by the Department of Economy and Tourism (DET). On the other hand, a Freezone company is limited to trading within that specific zone or internationally, but offers benefits like 100% tax exemptions and easier customs procedures.
How long does the company formation process take?
Our "Express Setup" can complete the initial trade license issuance in as little as 60 to 120 minutes for certain Freezones. However, a complete setup including corporate bank account and residency visas usually takes between 2 to 4 weeks depending on the complexity and government approvals.
Do I need a local UAE partner for my business?
No, the UAE laws have changed significantly. Now, for most commercial and industrial activities, foreigners can own 100% of their Mainland business without needing a UAE national as a 51% partner. You only need a UAE national as a "Local Service Agent" for professional licenses.
