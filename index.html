FAQs on Business & Licenses

What are the benefits of Free Zone companies in Sharjah?
Benefits include 100% foreign ownership, 100% repatriation of capital and profits, 0% corporate and personal income tax, and world-class infrastructure with no customs duties on import/export.
What is the process for setting up a business in a Sharjah Free Zone?
The process involves choosing your business activity, registering your trade name, applying for the license, submitting required documents (Passport, Photo, NOC), and obtaining your visa.
How much does it cost to start a business in UAE?
Starting costs vary. A Free Zone Flexi-Desk setup starts around AED 8,500 - 12,000, while Mainland LLC setups usually start from AED 16,000 to AED 25,000 depending on the license type.
Can a foreigner start a business in UAE?
Yes, foreigners can own 100% of their business in Free Zones and now also in many sectors within the UAE Mainland without needing a local partner.
Can you own 100% of a company in UAE?
Absolutely. Recent changes in UAE law allow 100% foreign ownership for over 1,000 commercial and industrial activities on the Mainland, besides the standard 100% ownership in all Free Zones.
Do small businesses need a license in the UAE?
Yes, every business entity in the UAE, regardless of size, must have a valid Trade License to operate legally. You can choose a Freelance Permit if you are a sole operator.
